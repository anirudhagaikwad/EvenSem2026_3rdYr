## Numerical / Calculation-based Questions

31. In a project, activity A (3 days) must finish before B (4 days) and C (5 days) can start. B must finish before D (2 days) starts. C must finish before D starts. Draw the network and find the critical path and project duration.

32. For the following activities, draw a PERT network and calculate expected duration and variance for each activity:  
   Activity | Optimistic (to) | Most Likely (tm) | Pessimistic (tp)  
   A        | 2               | 5                | 8  
   B        | 3               | 4                | 5  
   C        | 1               | 2                | 9  

33. Given the following CPM network data, determine the critical path and total project duration:  
   Activity | Predecessor | Duration (days)  
   A        | —           | 6  
   B        | A           | 8  
   C        | A           | 4  
   D        | B           | 5  
   E        | C           | 3  
   F        | D, E        | 7  

34. A project has a normal duration of 25 weeks at a cost of ₹18 lakh. The crash data for selected activities is given below. What is the minimum cost to crash the project to 22 weeks?

   Activity | Normal Duration | Crash Duration | Normal Cost | Crash Cost Increment  
   X        | 10              | 7              | 4.0         | 1.2 lakh per week  
   Y        | 8               | 5              | 3.2         | 0.9 lakh per week  
   Z        | 6               | 4              | 2.5         | 1.0 lakh per week  

35. Calculate the **slack** for every activity in the following small network:  
   Activity | Duration | Predecessors  
   A        | 4        | —  
   B        | 6        | A  
   C        | 3        | A

36. In PERT, an activity has to = 4, tm = 7, tp = 16 days. Calculate:
